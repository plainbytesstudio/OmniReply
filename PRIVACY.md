# Privacy Policy for OmniReply

**Effective Date:** March 2026  
**Developer:** PlainBytes Studio  
**GitHub:** [https://github.com/plainbytesstudio/OmniReply](https://github.com/plainbytesstudio/OmniReply)

At **PlainBytes Studio**, we prioritize your privacy. **OmniReply** is built as a client-side tool to ensure you have full control over your data.

---

### 1. Direct Data Transmission (No Intermediary Servers)
OmniReply does **not** operate any intermediary servers. 
- **Direct-to-API:** When you generate a reply, your clipboard content is sent **directly** from your local machine to the third-party AI provider you have configured (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek).
- **No Interception:** PlainBytes Studio **never** intercepts, collects, or stores your clipboard data, prompts, or generated responses. We have zero access to the conversations you have with AI models.

### 2. Third-Party AI Services
By using OmniReply, you acknowledge that your data will be processed by the AI providers you select.
- **Provider Policies:** Your data is subject to the privacy policies and data handling terms of the respective AI service (OpenAI, Google, xAI, or DeepSeek). 
- **API Keys:** Your API keys are stored locally on your device. OmniReply only uses them to authenticate your direct requests to the AI providers.

### 3. Local-Only Persistent Storage
All data that is "at rest" remains on your device:
- **Configurations:** Your Profiles, Rules, and Model settings are saved in local JSON files.
- **History Logs:** If the history feature is enabled, "Input-Output" pairs are saved exclusively in a local JSONL file. 
- **User Control:** You can manually inspect, modify, or delete these local files at any time.

### 4. No Telemetry or Analytics
OmniReply is a "clean" application. It does **not** contain any hidden telemetry, usage tracking, or analytics code. We do not know how many users have installed the app or which models are being used.

### 5. Intellectual Property & GitHub Presence
OmniReply is **Proprietary and Confidential** software owned by PlainBytes Studio. 
- **Non-Open Source:** Although the project has a presence on GitHub for distribution and issue tracking, the source code is not open-source. 
- **Usage Restrictions:** Users are prohibited from reverse-engineering, decompiling, or redistributing the application binaries without express written permission.

### 6. Automated Typing (Simulated Input)
The "Write-back" feature performs local keyboard simulation to enter text into your active window. This process is entirely local to your operating system and involves no external data transfer.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The latest version will always be available on our GitHub repository.
